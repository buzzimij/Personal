Executive Summary

Key Finding

  • Big 4 are 2–3 years ahead — Deloitte's agentic Omnia and PwC's $1B NGA platform are production systems, not prototypes
  • EisnerAmper is the biggest outlier: ranked AT #13–15 but scores #6 overall — their Azure Foundry Audit Design Agent is the most documented mid-market proprietary tool deployed
  • Cherry Bekaert and Armanino punch well above their revenue rank — both have real deployed tooling and aggressive 2026 roadmaps
  • CBIZ/Marcum and Forvis Mazars are the biggest disappointments relative to size — no audit-specific AI platform despite billion-dollar revenues

Tool Landscape

  • Proprietary builds: Deloitte (Omnia/DARTbot), PwC (NGA/Data PRO), EY (Canvas/Helix/GLAD), KPMG (Clara), RSM (Luca/Ask Luca), Grant Thornton (gtap), Crowe (CroweMind/Clarity), EisnerAmper (EisnerAI)
  • Vendor-first stacks: Baker Tilly (Fieldguide + DataSnipper + Ideagen), Cherry Bekaert (MindBridge since 2020), Armanino (Audit Ally + DataSnipper), Aprio (TimeCredit AI + Fieldguide)
  • Emerging/in-development: Citrin Cooperman (agentic workpaper agents), Wipfli (Power Platform audit apps)

Career Intelligence

  • Best employer for audit methodology + AI leadership: Deloitte, PwC, or EY — largest platforms, most defined roles, and clearest career paths for methodology professionals
  • Best non-Big-4 bet: EisnerAmper (#6) — proprietary platform already deployed, Azure partnership, fastest-growing partner class
  • Highest-upside wildcard: Grant Thornton (gtap just launched May 2026) or Baker Tilly (no platform yet — someone needs to build it)
  • PCAOB warning: BDO (60% deficiency) and RSM (41%) have real audit quality gaps despite major AI investment — verify how tools are actually used
