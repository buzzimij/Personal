Section 01

Executive Summary

Seven key findings from across the Top 25 US accounting firms, curated for a CPA with national office audit methodology experience who is evaluating employers for a Director/Partner-level AI + Audit leadership role over a 3–5 year horizon.

Merger Note: Baker Tilly and Moss Adams completed their combination on June 3, 2025, forming the 6th-largest US CPA firm by revenue (~$3.1B combined), backed by Hellman & Friedman at a $7B valuation. They are treated as a single entity throughout this report under the Baker Tilly name.
1
The Big 4 have each committed $1B+ to AI in audit. PwC ($1B AI-native audit platform targeting end-to-end automation by calendar 2026), EY ($1B Assurance technology investment with April 2026 enterprise-scale agentic AI rollout), KPMG ($2B Microsoft alliance with KPMG Clara and Workbench), and Deloitte (multi-billion AI transformation with Omnia platform expansion) are not just announcing — they are deploying. These are leadership-seat factories for AI + audit right now.
2
RSM is the most compelling mid-market play. A $1B three-year tech investment, a fully operational AI audit ecosystem (RSM Luca + Ask Luca launched January 2026), 4,000+ assurance professionals, and a dedicated agentic AI assurance roadmap. RSM is the only non-Big-4 firm with this level of audit-specific AI specificity and investment at scale.
3
Private equity is the accelerant. 10+ of the top 30 firms are now PE-backed. Baker Tilly (H&F), Grant Thornton (New Mountain Capital), Citrin Cooperman (Blackstone), Cherry Bekaert (Parthenon), Wipfli (New Mountain Capital), Sikich (Bain Capital), Armanino (Further Global), and CRI (Centerbridge) all carry PE capital. PE-backed firms move faster on tech investment — but also face more pressure on utilization, which creates urgency for AI that reduces headcount-to-revenue ratios.
4
Grant Thornton just crossed a critical threshold. The May 2026 launch of GTAP (Grant Thornton Analytics & Automation Platform) — built from scratch as the intelligent core of audit delivery — combined with New Mountain Capital backing and a $1B+ advisor AI investment, puts GT firmly in the tier just below the Big 4 for audit AI trajectory. The leadership role here is being built in real time.
5
PCAOB inspection pressure correlates with AI urgency. Grant Thornton (48% Part I.A deficiency rate, 2024) and RSM (41%) face the most acute quality pressure among annually-inspected firms. Firms under PCAOB scrutiny have direct board-level incentive to deploy AI as a quality remedy — which translates to leadership seats with real mandate and budget.
6
EisnerAmper punches above its weight. The March 2026 launch of the EisnerAI Audit Design Agent — built in collaboration with Microsoft Foundry — deployed across all EisnerAmper audits in 2026 makes this a surprise over-performer for a firm its size. For a Director-level role where you can own the entire program rather than be a layer inside a Big 4 bureaucracy, EisnerAmper deserves serious consideration.
7
The bottom tier is real. Several top-25 firms (Plante Moran, Wipfli, CohnReznick, Withum, PKF O'Connor Davies) show thought leadership content about AI but limited evidence of audit-specific deployment, proprietary tooling, or dedicated AI audit leadership roles. For someone with your background, these are lifestyle choices, not career accelerators — unless a PE-backed transformation is imminent.
Section 02

Scoring Methodology

Each firm is scored on 100 points across five weighted dimensions, calibrated specifically for a senior CPA evaluating long-term employer fit for an AI + audit methodology leadership role. Being fully deployed today is NOT the primary criterion — trajectory, commitment depth, and the existence or creation of leadership seats matters more.

25 pts
Strategic Commitment to AI in Audit
Leadership public commitments, dollar amounts announced, board-level buy-in, timeline specificity. Are they putting money where the press release is?
25 pts
Audit-Specific AI Trajectory
Are tools being built for assurance specifically — risk assessment, sampling, workpaper automation, anomaly detection — or is this generic AI / tax/advisory work? Audit-specific deployment scores higher.
20 pts
Hiring & Talent Investment
Active job postings for AI + audit roles, recent hires of AI/tech leaders in the assurance practice, whether a leadership seat exists or is being created. Firm headcount growth trajectory.
15 pts
Firm Health & Growth
Revenue growth rate, PE backing (as a proxy for investment velocity), merger momentum, balance sheet stability, and market position trajectory.
15 pts
Innovation Culture
Speed of adoption, number and depth of external technology partnerships, dedicated AI labs or innovation centers, evidence that the firm rewards experimentation and tolerates the cost of change.
