Section 02

What Is Verity?

Verity is CaseWare's AI intelligence and orchestration layer — announced May 20, 2026 and officially launched at the CwX 2026 conference in Fort Lauderdale (May 21–22, 2026). It is described as the "industry-first agentic solution specifically designed for assurance professionals."

The Core Concept — "The First AI That Understands the Engagement"

Most AI tools in audit sit outside the file — you paste content in, get a response, copy it back. Verity is built inside the engagement. It has access to the full context of the audit file: prior year workpapers, board minutes, financial statements, risk assessments, client history. This engagement-context awareness is CaseWare's primary differentiator claim.

Section 03

The Three AI Agents

At launch, Verity consists of three distinct agents, each targeting a specific phase of the audit workflow. They are not interchangeable — each has a defined task scope and a defined place in the engagement timeline.

AGENT 01
Disclosure Checklist Agent
GA — Live in DAS

Automates the financial statement disclosure review process. Reviews disclosures against applicable standards, flags gaps, and generates citation-backed suggestions the auditor can accept, refine, or override before posting to the workpaper.

Where it lives: Embedded in DAS during the completion/reporting phase of the engagement.

Key outcome: Replaces a largely manual checklist process that currently involves cross-referencing disclosures against GAAP/GAAS requirements.

2.7 hrs saved per workflow
AGENT 02
Document Intelligence Agent
Closed Beta

Automates document review, analysis, and evidence extraction directly into workpapers. Ingests client-provided documents (contracts, agreements, statements) and extracts relevant information into the appropriate workpaper location.

Where it lives: Fieldwork phase — populates evidence in workpapers from document uploads.

Underlying tech: Powered in part by the Extractly.ai acquisition (Jan 2025), which specialized in PDF extraction and financial statement parsing.

~80% document review time reduction (est.)
AGENT 03
Risk Suggestion Agent
Alpha

Analyzes multi-year financial data combined with board minutes and prior-year risk documentation to generate risk suggestions at the planning stage. Surfaces risks the engagement team may not have explicitly identified.

Where it lives: Planning phase — generates a suggested risk register that teams can accept, modify, or supplement.

Why this matters: Risk identification quality is highly auditor-dependent; this aims to standardize and elevate the floor across all engagements and experience levels.

Planning earliest in audit lifecycle
The Full Workflow Coverage Ambition

Taken together, the three agents cover planning → fieldwork → completion. This is deliberate — CaseWare is signaling that Verity will eventually provide AI assistance across the entire engagement lifecycle, not just isolated tasks. The Risk Agent handles planning, Document Intelligence handles fieldwork, and Disclosure Checklist handles the completion/reporting phase.

Section 04

DAS Integration — The Full Picture

Direct Answer: Yes — Verity's Home Is DAS

Verity is built into DAS. The Disclosure Checklist Agent is generally available inside DAS right now. The other two agents will also launch inside DAS first. This is not a light integration — Verity uses DAS's engagement data (prior year files, risk registers, financial statements) as the context that powers the AI. DAS is the reason Verity can claim to "understand the engagement."

What Is DAS?

DAS (Dynamic Audit Solution) is a cloud-native, end-to-end audit platform co-developed by AICPA + CPA.com + CaseWare. It was commercially launched in Summer 2023. It is the most significant product in CaseWare's current portfolio and the center of their growth strategy.

Why the AICPA Partnership Matters

The AICPA co-development is not just a marketing badge. It means:

  • The audit methodology is baked into the workflow — the "right" procedures surface at the right time based on assessed risk
  • Standard-setting changes flow through more quickly (AICPA has a stake in keeping DAS current)
  • AICPA endorses DAS in materials to member firms — implicit channel support
  • Verity's AI agents are built on top of this methodology, meaning risk suggestions and disclosure checklists are calibrated to AICPA standards
Important Limitation to Flag

If your firm is not on DAS (i.e., still on Working Papers / CaseWare Hybrid), you do not have access to Verity agents today. There is no announced timeline for Verity availability in Working Papers or the hybrid environment. This is a migration forcing function — Verity is an incentive to move to the cloud/DAS platform.

Why CaseWare Uses Bedrock / Claude

  • Enterprise data controls: Amazon Bedrock ensures client engagement data does not leave CaseWare's controlled environment and is not used to train foundation models — a critical requirement for audit data containing MNPI and confidential financial information
  • Anthropic Claude is favored by compliance-sensitive industries for its Constitutional AI design and lower hallucination rates vs. alternatives on structured financial tasks
  • Citation capability — the citation-backed output design aligns with Claude's retrieval and attribution strengths
What "Agentic" Actually Means Here

Verity agents are not simple prompt-and-response. An agent is a system that can plan, use tools, and take multi-step actions toward a goal. In Verity's case, the Disclosure Checklist Agent likely: (1) retrieves the financial statements, (2) identifies disclosure requirements from standards, (3) compares actual disclosures against requirements, (4) drafts gap findings with citations, (5) presents output for human review. This multi-step, tool-using architecture is what separates Verity from a chatbot overlay.

How CaseWare Positions Verity

CaseWare's primary competitive messaging attacks "disconnected copilots" and "standalone AI assistants" — tools that sit outside the engagement workflow and require the auditor to manually transfer context. Verity's claimed moat is engagement context awareness: because it lives inside DAS, it already knows the client, the risks, the prior year, and the engagement history.

The AICPA partnership is the hardest to replicate — it creates a standards-alignment advantage that pure-play AI tools cannot match.

Section 11

Strategic Implications for the Firm

If the Firm Is Currently on Working Papers / Hybrid

  • Verity is not available to you today. There is no announced path to bring Verity agents to Working Papers.
  • The gap between desktop users and DAS/cloud users in terms of AI capability will widen significantly over the next 12–24 months.
  • This is a meaningful input to any ongoing platform evaluation — the AI roadmap is cloud-only.
  • If a DAS evaluation is not already underway, the question of when (not if) should be on the agenda.

If the Firm Is on DAS

  • Request Verity access now. The Disclosure Checklist Agent is GA. If you are on DAS, your CaseWare rep should be able to activate it.
  • Identify a pilot engagement for the Disclosure Checklist Agent — a straightforward GAAP audit with standard disclosures is ideal for the first run.
  • Consider applying for the Document Intelligence Agent closed beta if your firm processes high volumes of third-party documents (lease abstracts, loan agreements, customer contracts).
  • Track time savings from the Disclosure Checklist Agent — the 2.7 hr/workflow figure gives a benchmark; your own data will be more credible for internal business case purposes.

Talent & Training Considerations

  • Verity's human-in-the-loop design means auditors must be trained to critically evaluate AI output, not just accept it. Professional skepticism applies to AI suggestions as it does to client representations.
  • The Risk Suggestion Agent (alpha) has the highest professional judgment requirement — teams will need to understand when AI-generated risks are appropriate vs. generic.
  • Senior staff (managers/supervisors) reviewing AI-assisted workpapers will need guidance on what constitutes adequate review of AI-prepared content.
  • Standard-setters (PCAOB, AICPA) are actively developing guidance on AI use in audit — the firm's methodology group should monitor this closely.

Quality Management Angle

  • Under SQMS No. 1 (effective December 2025), firms are required to document technology risks in their quality management system. AI audit tools — including Verity — should be in scope of your firm's technology risk assessment.
  • CaseWare's SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications provide the third-party assurance documentation firms will need for their own quality management files.
  • Consider how AI-assisted engagement steps should be documented in workpapers — regulators are beginning to ask about this in inspection contexts.
