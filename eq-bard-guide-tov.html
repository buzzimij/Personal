Haste & DPS Testing
Key Findings — Empirically Tested
Winner
Verses of Victory beats McVaxius for DPS — VoV gives 30% haste vs McVaxius 22%. Extra 103 hits over 30 minutes (+9% DPS) overwhelms McVaxius ATK+14 advantage (+2/hit avg damage). Use VoV by default.
Confirmed
Brass horn has zero impact on swing speed. McVaxius swings/min identical with and without horn (36.0). Horn only boosts STR/ATK/DS secondary effects — roughly +2 avg damage per hit.
Confirmed
Composition of Ervaj stacks on top of both songs in its own unique haste slot. Adds ~8–9% more swings/min on top of McVaxius or VoV.
Caveat
McVaxius is better only if already haste capped from item haste + enchanter Celerity. At cap, VoV haste is wasted and McVaxius ATK+14/STR matters more.

Haste Mechanics — Three Stacking Categories
Rule
Three separate haste categories all stack additively: Item haste (belt) + Caster spell haste (enchanter) + Bard song haste. Within each category, only the highest applies. Instrument modifier does NOT boost the haste % of any song — only secondary effects (STR/ATK/DS/AGI).

Modifier Formula
Formula
Song secondary effect = base value × instrument multiplier
Multiplier = resonance ÷ 10
Example: resonance 12 = 1.2× boost to STR/ATK/DS/AGI effects. Haste % is always flat and unaffected.

Drum barely boosts wind songs (Psalms) — nearly 1.0× on those. Puretone at 2.8× nearly doubles Psalm output. Epic at 1.8× would give Psalms ~mid-190s cold/fire (between drum and Puretone).
Why Psalms vs Elemental Rhythms?
No Epic (Current)
Use Elemental Rhythms over Psalms. Drum boosts Elemental well (percussion). Psalms are wind songs — drum gives near-zero boost. Elemental gives 157 fire / 160 cold vs Psalms only 137/140 with drum. Psalms only win with epic or Puretone.
With Epic
Swap to Psalms. Epic gives 1.8× to all types — now Psalms beat Elemental significantly. Stack Guardian + Psalm of X for targeted coverage. Pop Puretone on named pull to spike to 2.8×.
Note
Psalm of Mystical Shielding + Guardian both add MR — stack them to max magic resist. Elemental Rhythms also contributes MR, which is why Guardian + Elemental hits 251 MR vs Guardian alone at ~168.

TOV Dragon Quick Guide
Zone Overview
Zone Layout
Three wings: North (most dangerous — named dragons 66+), East (Halls of Testing), West (quest NPCs, Claws of Veeshan allies). Entry via clickable tower in Western Wastes. Most AEs are avoidable behind walls — use LoS. Target ~300 buffed fire and cold resist for trash.
North Wing — Named Dragons
Lord Vyemm
Magic AE Hardest in TOV
  • Arguably harder than Vulak — extreme melee damage
  • Guards: Hsrek and Lurian must be cleared first
  • Prioritize MR — Guardian + Psalm of Mystical Shielding
  • Slowable
Aaryonar
Cold AE Hard
  • Guards entrance to main ring of North wing
  • Will assist nearby dragons if others are aggroed first — pull carefully
  • Psalm of Warmth + Puretone on pull
Eashen of the Sky
Fire + Cold Unslowable
  • Casts Rain of Molten Lava (fire), Thunder Blast, Wave of Cold
  • Unslowable — can't reduce DPS intake
  • Multi-element — hard to fully resist both; prioritize cold
  • Healers need fast heals (Remedy)
Ikatiar the Venom
Poison/Disease Unslowable
  • Wyvern — rogue class, unslowable
  • Drops Ancient Wyvern Hide armor set
  • Poison/disease AE — Elemental Rhythms covers poison
Dagarn the Destroyer
Fire AE Level 70
  • Wurm — casts Rain of Molten Lava, Wave of Heat, Call of the Zero
  • ~200 point fire PBAE — prioritize fire resist
  • Slowable, rampages
  • Psalm of Cooling + Puretone on pull (with epic)
Lord Feshlak
Fire + Cold Medium
  • Casts Wave of Cold and Wave of Fire — both elements
  • Gates — bring a snare bard song or root
  • Multi-element is awkward — use Guardian + Elemental for balanced coverage
Lady Mirenilla
Cold + Magic Medium
  • Casts Call of the Zero, Cloud of Fear, Frost Strike
  • Slowable
  • Guards: Gra'Vloren and We'lWnas — clear first
  • Cold + MR priority
Lord Kreizenn
Cold AE Medium
  • Casts Wave of Flame (cold DD with zero-second stun AOE — misleadingly named)
  • Psalm of Warmth priority
Jorlleag
Cold AE Healer
  • Cleric class — casts Complete Heal and Gates
  • Casts Frost Breath (cold AE)
  • Slowable — requires 6 clerics on healing rotation
  • Interrupt the CH or fight drags on forever
Lady Nevederia
Magic Enchanter
  • Enchanter class — possible charm/mez abilities
  • Air dragon — "among the most fearsome"
  • MR priority
Lord Koi'Doken
Cold/Water Medium
  • Water dragon — massive sea dragon deep in temple
  • Cold resist priority
Lendiniara the Keeper
Magic AE Unslowable
  • Casts Silver Breath — unslowable
  • Quest NPC (CoV faction) — also a raid target
  • Oldest dragon alive (save Veeshan)
  • 12% spawn chance

Final Boss
Vulak'Aerr Level 70 — Final Boss
Disease PBAE Unresistable HP DoT
  • Hits for 1,200+ — permarooted, slowable
  • Flurries — melee takes heavy damage
  • Gift of Aerr: PBAE 50 HP DoT — unresistable
  • Ancient Breath: PBAE DoT, -40% Attack Speed, 250 HP DD/tick, disease-based
  • Call of the Zero: summons tank directly under boss
Bard Priority
Disease resist (Ancient Breath). Gift of Aerr is unresistable — keep healing rotation tight. VoV + Guardian + Psalm of Mystical Shielding recommended.
Trash Notes
Key Trash Mob Warnings
Shimmer Drake
Mass dispel — will strip the entire raid's buffs. Identify and kill fast. Fire/cold PBAE typical of zone trash.
Glimmer / Glimmering Drakes
"Glimmer = always a Druid (gates)" / "Glimmering = never gates." Know the naming to prep interrupt on gate.
General Trash
~300 buffed fire and cold resist recommended. Most AEs avoidable through walls — use line-of-sight. Without epic, Guardian + Elemental + drum is your balanced trash melody.
